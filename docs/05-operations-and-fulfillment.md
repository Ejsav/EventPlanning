# 05 · Operations & fulfillment plan

The website promises specific things. Operations exist to keep those promises: published fees, a lead planner introduced before signing who leads the day, at least two people on every wedding day, client pays vendors directly, disclosed referral relationships, a reply within one business day.

## Operating model
- **Company (Sound & Stone / New England Event Planners LLC):** brand, marketing, sales calls, proposals, contracts, client billing, shared documents (planning calendar, budget, vendor directory, floor plan, production schedule, run of show), transportation logistics, quality control.
- **Lead planners (independent contractors):** experienced professionals who lead planning meetings, vendor management and the wedding day under the company's standards and documents.
- **Assistants (contractors):** day-of support; trained on the company's run-of-show format.
- **Vendors:** contracted and paid directly by clients. The company recommends; it does not resell.

## Recruiting and vetting planners (before selling)
1. Source: experienced independent planners and venue coordinators in each region; planners who want weekday income without running a business; referrals from photographers and venues.
2. Screen against the published standard (see `/approach#who`): has led weddings as the lead (ask for 3 recent weddings with dates/venues), professional references (call them), professional liability insurance, availability calendar.
3. Paid trial: assist on one wedding, or run a mock run-of-show and timeline exercise.
4. Agreement: independent contractor agreement per wedding (scope, flat fee, payment timing after client payment, cancellation terms, non-solicitation of clients, confidentiality, standards, insurance requirements, photography/social-media rules).
5. **Never sell a date before a qualified lead planner has accepted it in writing.** Keep a bench: at least two planners per region before marketing heavily there.

## Delivery workflow (maps to the client-facing process)
| Stage | Owner | Output / SLA |
|---|---|---|
| Inquiry | Company | Personal reply within 1 business day; triage notes come with every lead email |
| Introductory call (30 min) | Company (+ planner for Full-Service) | Fit recommendation; date hold for 5 business days if mutually interested |
| Proposal | Company | Scope + flat fee + payment schedule within 2–3 days; introduce the lead planner |
| Agreement & retainer | Company | E-signed contract + retainer (40%/50%); planner confirmed for date |
| Onboarding | Company | Workspace + planning calendar opened within a week |
| Planning | Lead planner + company | Meetings per package; live budget; vendor directory |
| Final 8–12 weeks | Lead planner | Plan audit, confirmations, walkthrough, run of show to every vendor the week before |
| Wedding day | Lead + assistant(s) | Arrive first, leave last; emergency kit; personal items handed off |
| After | Company | Thank-you, review request (with permission), feedback survey, planner debrief |

## Preventing fulfillment failures
- **Backup planner** assigned for every wedding date; documents live in the shared workspace, not in a planner's inbox.
- **Weather plans** with decision deadlines agreed with vendors at the 4-week walkthrough.
- **Vendor changes:** if a client's vendor cancels, the planner sources alternatives within 48 hours; the client decides.
- **Planner illness/emergency:** backup steps in with the full run of show; the client is told immediately.
- **QA:** owner reviews every run of show the week before; post-wedding debrief within 7 days.

## Transportation (competitive advantage, kept in proportion)
Offer guest transportation coordination as an add-on and fold it into Full-Service planning. Source vehicles only from licensed, insured carriers, keep proof of operating authority and insurance on file, and route around the Merritt Parkway restriction on buses. If vehicles are ever brokered with a margin (for example through existing transportation relationships), **disclose that in writing** — it is a referral/resale relationship and the site promises disclosure.

## Items requiring qualified professional review (do not skip)
| Area | What to review | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Worker classification | Connecticut applies the strict "ABC test" for unemployment purposes; misclassifying planners/assistants as contractors carries real liability. Structure (or employ) accordingly. | Employment attorney + CPA |
| Client agreement | Scope, payment schedule, retainer refundability, cancellation/postponement, force majeure, limitation of liability, photo/social permissions, vendor-responsibility disclaimer | Attorney |
| Contractor agreement | As above, plus indemnity, insurance, IP and non-solicit | Attorney |
| Insurance | General liability and professional liability (E&O) for the company; certificates of insurance often required by venues; event cancellation insurance offered to clients as their choice | Insurance broker |
| Trade name & trademark | Trade name certificate for "Sound & Stone"; USPTO clearance before investing in print and signage | Attorney |
| Tax | Sales/use tax treatment of planning services in Connecticut, 1099 reporting, revenue recognition of retainers | CPA |
| Privacy | Privacy policy, data retention, marketing consent (CAN-SPAM), Connecticut Data Privacy Act applicability | Attorney |
| Referral fees | Disclosure language for any vendor referral arrangements | Attorney |

## First paying customers (practical plan)
1. Sign two lead planners (one Fairfield County, one shoreline/New Haven) and two assistants.
2. Lead with **Wedding Management** for 2027 dates — the easiest promise to keep and the fastest way to build reviews and vendor relationships.
3. Meet 10 venue coordinators and 10 photographers in person in the first 60 days (see [08](08-marketing-plan.md)); bring a printed one-page "how we work with venues" sheet.
4. Offer an introductory **launch rate on a limited number of 2027 Wedding Management dates** only if needed — state it plainly as a launch rate (never as a fake discount from an inflated price).
5. Document every wedding (with permission) so the site gains real proof within the first season.
