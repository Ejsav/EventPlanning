**Observations about competitors (from listings and search results):**
- Most established Fairfield County luxury planners **do not publish prices** and lead with portfolios and press. Price transparency is rare.
- Many smaller planners are owner-operators whose brand is their personal name. Capacity is limited by one person's Saturdays.
- Inquiry experiences are typically a long single-page form or a marketplace message thread; few explain what happens after inquiring.
- Day-of offerings vary wildly in what "day-of" means (some start 4–8 weeks out, some on the morning).
- Trust signals are dominated by reviews and real-wedding galleries — which a new brand cannot (and must not) fake.

## Where the opportunity is

1. **Transparency as a differentiator.** Publishing flat starting fees, a written-proposal promise and a clear process removes the biggest friction in this category — "can we even afford to call?" — and lets the site qualify visitors before they inquire.
2. **Systems over personality.** A brand-led company (not a single planner's name) can scale across Saturdays by contracting multiple vetted lead planners while keeping one standard, one set of documents and one client experience.
3. **Logistics credibility.** The operator's real background in event transportation and vendor coordination is uncommon among planners and maps directly to pain points couples have (shuttles, rural venues, multi-site weddings). It is used as a supporting proof point, never as the headline.
4. **Regional specificity.** Thin "Connecticut wedding planner" pages abound; genuinely useful regional pages (Merritt Parkway bus restrictions, Yale commencement weekends, Litchfield cell coverage) can rank and convert.

## Positioning

> **Sound & Stone plans and runs Connecticut weddings with published pricing, written plans and a lead planner you meet before you sign — so couples can enjoy the romance while we carry the structure.**

- **Category:** Wedding planning & wedding management (Connecticut, expanding to New England).
- **For:** Couples (and often parents) planning $50k–$200k+ weddings in Fairfield County, the shoreline, New Haven, Hartford County and the Litchfield Hills, who value calm, clarity and taste.
- **Against:** Opaque luxury planners (price-on-application) and inconsistent budget day-of coordinators.
- **Reasons to believe (all deliverable today):** published fees; flat-fee proposals; two-person minimum on every wedding day; lead planner introduced pre-signing; client pays vendors directly; disclosed referral relationships; reply within one business day.

## Service strategy (what to sell first)

| Service | Demand | Fulfillment difficulty | Margin (est.) | Role in launch |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wedding Management (month-of+) | Highest; many couples DIY then panic | Lowest — needs 1 experienced coordinator + 1 assistant | ~37% before marketing | **Lead offer.** Builds volume, reviews and vendor relationships fastest. |
| Partial Planning | Strong; couples with a venue who are overwhelmed | Moderate | ~44% | **Core profit product.** |
| Full-Service Planning | Lower volume, highest ticket | Highest — needs a senior lead planner and design capability | ~40% | **Accept selectively** once a proven senior lead planner is contracted. |
| Guest transportation coordination | Add-on; high relevance for rural/multi-site | Low for this operator | ~60% | **Differentiating add-on**, never the headline. |
| Rehearsal dinner / welcome party / brunch | Add-on | Low–moderate | Good | Weekend upsell. |
| Premium concierge | Niche | Hard to define & deliver | — | **Not offered at launch.** |

Full-Service is published (it anchors value and attracts high-intent search), but the operating plan in [05](05-operations-and-fulfillment.md) is explicit: do not sign a full-service client until a qualified senior lead planner has agreed to that date in writing.

## Key assumptions to validate in the first 90 days
- Contractor lead-planner rates in CT at the levels modeled in [04](04-service-economics.md).
- Inquiry → booking conversion with transparent pricing (track it; don't assume it).
- Whether $3,200 Wedding Management is accepted given its two-person staffing and 10–12-week start (market range $1,650–$4,000).
